# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Developers, creators, and small teams who manage short links, custom slugs, and link performance.

## Product Purpose

TinyTags is a URL management product focused on fast link operations, clear ownership boundaries, and practical analytics. It should help users complete link workflows with minimal friction and high trust.

## Brand Personality

Confident, precise, modern-technical.

## Anti-references

- Decorative, playful consumer-social UI patterns that distract from utility
- Over-animated interfaces that delay tasks
- Multi-accent, ornamental visual systems that weaken hierarchy

## Design Principles

1. Task-first UI: interface recedes so links and analytics stay primary.
2. Consistent system grammar: shared tokens, spacing, radius, and component behavior across surfaces.
3. Intentional signaling: accent and motion are used for interaction/state, never decoration.
4. Trust through predictability: auth, ownership, and editing flows behave consistently.
5. Accessible by default: readable contrast, visible focus, and reduced-motion support.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for core product flows, preserve strong focus visibility, keep body text contrast at AA levels, and support `prefers-reduced-motion` for animated transitions.
